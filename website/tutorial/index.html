Ask your database anything.

Connect a MySQL database, choose an AI model, and ask questions in plain English. No SQL needed.

  1. 1Connect your database
  2. 2Choose your AI
  3. 3Ask questions

About 10 minutes. You’ll need Quill installed. Download it here.

1

Connect your database

Quill works with nearly 90 kinds of databases. This guide uses MySQL. The steps are the same for the others.

Choose MySQL

Click New Connection at the top of the window. Choose MySQL, then click Next.

Enter your details

Give the connection a name you’ll recognize, like “Shop”. Then fill in the details for your database. Whoever looks after your database, such as your IT team or hosting provider, can give you these.

Host
The address of the database server. On your own computer it’s 127.0.0.1.
Port
Usually 3306 for MySQL.
User and password
Your database login.
Database
Which database to open, for example shop.

Test, then connect

Click Test. When you see Connection successful, click Save & Connect. Your database now appears in the list on the left.

No data yet? Use our sample shop.

It’s a small online store with customers, products, orders, payments and reviews, so you can try everything safely.

  1. Download the sample shop.
  2. In Quill, right-click your database and choose SQL and code, then Execute SQL File.
  3. Click Browse, choose the file you downloaded, then click Execute.

Have a look around

Open your database and its Tables in the list on the left. Double-click a table to see its rows. Nothing you look at changes your data.

2

Choose your AI

Quill works with the AI service you prefer. Pick one below, or ask your IT team which one your company uses.

Open AI settings

Click the gear in the top-right corner, then AI, then Add Config. Choose your AI service from the Provider list.

Claude

Best for accurate answers. Made by Anthropic.

  1. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.
  2. Set Provider to Claude and paste the key into API Key.
  3. Set Default Model to claude-sonnet-5-5.

OpenAI

The company behind ChatGPT.

  1. Get a key at platform.openai.com.
  2. Set Provider to OpenAI and paste the key into API Key.
  3. Pick a Default Model from the list Quill shows you.

Ollama

Free and private. The AI runs on your own computer, so nothing leaves it.

  1. Install Ollama and download a model, such as qwen2.5:7b.
  2. Set Provider to Ollama. Leave API Key empty.
  3. Set Default Model to the model you downloaded.

Smaller models on a laptop are slower and less reliable. For important numbers, use the Query Data option in part 3, or a larger model.

OpenRouter

One account for models from many companies.

  1. Get a key at openrouter.ai/keys.
  2. Set Provider to OpenAI Compatible and Endpoint to https://openrouter.ai/api/v1.
  3. Paste your key, and copy a model name from openrouter.ai/models.

LiteLLM

For companies that route AI through their own gateway. Your IT team gives you the details.

  1. Set Provider to OpenAI Compatible.
  2. Set Endpoint to your gateway address, for example http://localhost:4000.
  3. Paste the key your IT team gave you and the model name they use.
For your IT team: a minimal LiteLLM setup 
pip install 'litellm[proxy]'
# litellm.yaml
model_list:
  - model_name: claude
    litellm_params:
      model: anthropic/claude-sonnet-5-5
      api_key: os.environ/ANTHROPIC_API_KEY

general_settings:
  master_key: sk-quill-local
litellm --config litellm.yaml   # serves on port 4000

In Quill, use the master_key as the API key and claude as the model.

Test, then apply

Click Test at the bottom. When you see Connection successful, click Apply. You can add more than one and switch between them later.

3

Ask questions

Now the fun part. Ask the way you’d ask a colleague who knows the data.

Open the assistant

Select your database on the left, then click the robot icon at the top right.

At the bottom of the assistant, choose Agent, then Query Data. In this mode Quill looks up the real numbers instead of guessing.

Ask in plain English

Type your question and press Enter. For example:

“Which products made the most money this year?”

Here’s what Quill did with that question:

  1. Found the right tables. It matched “products” and “money” to the products, orders and order items tables.
  2. Checked it was safe. The query only reads data, so it ran straight away.
  3. Answered with real numbers from your database.

The answer is from a small AI model running on a laptop. Larger models give richer answers.

Questions to try

Sales

  • How much did we sell each month this year?
  • What share of orders was refunded?
  • Which sales channel brings in the most revenue?

Customers

  • Who are our 10 best customers?
  • How many customers do we have in each country?
  • Which customers haven’t ordered in 90 days?

Products

  • Which products are almost out of stock?
  • What’s the average review score for each category?
  • Which products get the worst reviews?

Get better answers

  • Say what you mean. “Revenue from paid orders only” is clearer than “revenue”.
  • Give a time range. “Last 90 days” or “in 2026” avoids guessing.
  • Ask follow-ups. “Now split that by country” builds on the last answer.
  • Check the numbers that matter. Click the steps above an answer to see exactly what Quill ran.

Your data stays safe

  • Looking is safe. Questions only read your data.
  • Changes need your OK. If a request would change or delete anything, Quill shows you exactly what it will do and waits for you to approve.
  • Lock it down. Mark a connection as Read Only in its settings, or ask your IT team for a read-only login.
  • Keep it private. Only the few tables a question needs are shared with your AI service. With Ollama, nothing leaves your computer.

If something doesn’t work

Test says it can’t connect
Check the host, port, user and password with whoever looks after the database. If the database is on another network, you may need a VPN or the SSH option in the connection window.
The assistant says AI isn’t configured
Add a config in Settings, then AI, and make sure you clicked Apply.
The AI says the key is invalid
Copy the key again, without spaces. Keys from your AI service are different from your database password.
The answer has SQL but no numbers
Switch the assistant to Agent, then Query Data, and ask again.
